# Privacy policy: AI Crawler Check

Last updated: 25 November 2026

AI Crawler Check is a Chrome extension published by Cituna (Eternum Capital FZE LLC).

**It collects no personal data and no browsing data.** Nothing is sent to Cituna or to any third party.

What it does when you click its icon on a page:

- It asks that same website for `/robots.txt`, `/llms.txt` and the current page's response headers.
- It reads the page's meta robots tags, canonical link and JSON-LD structured data.
- It shows the result in the popup. Nothing is stored, logged or transmitted.

Permissions:

- `activeTab`: lets the extension read the tab you are on, only after you click its icon.
- `scripting`: runs the read-only check above in that tab.

The extension has no analytics, no cookies of its own, no accounts and no remote code. The "See what AI engines say" button opens cituna.com in a new tab; what happens there is covered by Cituna's privacy policy at https://cituna.com/privacy.
